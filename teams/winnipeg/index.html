Winnipeg Jets Hockey Broadcast Rights & Regional Blackout Guide (2026–27 Jets Season)

Following the Winnipeg Jets regular season and preseason broadcasts across Canada requires understanding how NHL broadcast territory rights are allocated between national networks and regional partners. For Winnipeg Jets fans, games are split across five distinct television and streaming networks: TSN3, , Sportsnet, Amazon Prime Video, and TVA Sports.

Regional Winnipeg Jets Games (TSN3 & )

TSN3 (50 English games) hold the official regional rights for the Winnipeg Jets. Their broadcast boundary is restricted by NHL carriage rules strictly to Manitoba, Saskatchewan, NW Ontario.

If you reside outside this territory, standard cable subscriptions to TSN3 will show a regional blackout screen during live Winnipeg Jets games. Out-of-market Jets fans must use Sportsnet+ Premium (for online streaming) or add NHL Centre Ice (for traditional cable/satellite TV) to watch regional feeds legally. Note that NHL Centre Ice does not include an online streaming option.

National Jets Games (Sportsnet, Prime & TVA Sports)

When the Winnipeg Jets play on Saturday night Hockey Night in Canada broadcasts, Sportsnet (English) and TVA Sports (French) broadcast coast-to-coast with zero regional blackouts anywhere in Canada. Every Canadian viewer with standard cable or basic streaming packages can watch these matchups.

Select regular-season Winnipeg Jets games stream nationally on Wednesday nights exclusively on Amazon Prime Video in English, which also carries coast-to-coast clearance with no regional blackouts.

Quick Summary for Jets Fans Living Outside Manitoba/Saskatchewan: If tonight's Winnipeg Jets game is scheduled on Sportsnet or Prime Video, you can watch anywhere in Canada without blackouts. If the game is on TSN3, you will be blacked out in BC, Alberta, and Eastern Canada unless you have Sportsnet+ Premium or NHL Centre Ice.

Why are Jets games blacked out in Ontario?
The NHL broadcast territory for the Jets covers Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Nunavut, and Northwestern Ontario (including Thunder Bay). If you live further east in Ontario (like Toronto or Ottawa), you are in the Leafs or Senators home territory. In that zone, regional Jets games on TSN3 are blacked out to protect local rights unless you subscribe to Sportsnet+ Premium or NHL Centre Ice.

Why am I getting a Sportsnet blackout or NHL regional blackout tonight?

If your screen goes dark and displays a blackout message on Sportsnet or TSN, the game is a Regional Broadcast and your physical location is outside the team's NHL-defined territory. You must use Sportsnet+ Premium to stream out-of-market regional games.

Even if you pay for the channel through a standard TV package, the NHL forces the network to blackout the signal for out-of-market viewers.

How do I watch Winnipeg Jets games in Canada without regional blackouts?

Out-of-market fans living outside the team's regional territory can watch all regional Winnipeg Jets games on TSN3 and by subscribing to Sportsnet+ Premium or NHL Centre Ice. This bypasses the standard blackout restrictions for standard cable subscribers.

Are Saturday night Winnipeg Jets games blacked out anywhere in Canada?

No. Saturday night Winnipeg Jets games on Sportsnet and TVA Sports are part of the NHL's national broadcast package. These games clear coast-to-coast across Canada with zero regional blackouts on standard cable packages.

Which Winnipeg Jets games stream exclusively on Amazon Prime Video?

National Wednesday Night Hockey broadcasts featuring the Winnipeg Jets are exclusive to Amazon Prime Video in English across Canada. These streams carry no regional blackout restrictions for Prime subscribers anywhere in the country.
