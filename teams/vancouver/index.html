Vancouver Canucks Hockey Broadcast Rights & Regional Blackout Guide (2026–27 Canucks Season)

Following the Vancouver Canucks regular season and preseason broadcasts across Canada requires understanding how NHL broadcast territory rights are allocated between national networks and regional partners. For Vancouver Canucks fans, games are split across five distinct television and streaming networks: Sportsnet Pacific, , Sportsnet, Amazon Prime Video, and TVA Sports.

Regional Vancouver Canucks Games (Sportsnet Pacific & )

Sportsnet Pacific (36 English games) hold the official regional rights for the Vancouver Canucks. Their broadcast boundary is restricted by NHL carriage rules strictly to British Columbia and Yukon.

If you reside outside this territory, standard cable subscriptions to Sportsnet Pacific will show a regional blackout screen during live Vancouver Canucks games. Out-of-market Canucks fans must use Sportsnet+ Premium (for online streaming) or add NHL Centre Ice (for traditional cable/satellite TV) to watch regional feeds legally. Note that NHL Centre Ice does not include an online streaming option.

National Canucks Games (Sportsnet, Prime & TVA Sports)

When the Vancouver Canucks play on Saturday night Hockey Night in Canada broadcasts, Sportsnet (English) and TVA Sports (French) broadcast coast-to-coast with zero regional blackouts anywhere in Canada. Every Canadian viewer with standard cable or basic streaming packages can watch these matchups.

Select regular-season Vancouver Canucks games stream nationally on Wednesday nights exclusively on Amazon Prime Video in English, which also carries coast-to-coast clearance with no regional blackouts.

Why are Canucks games blacked out in Alberta?
The NHL broadcast boundary for the Canucks is drawn at the British Columbia border. The Canucks' home territory covers strictly BC and the Yukon. If you live in Alberta, Saskatchewan, or the NWT, you are in the Flames/Oilers territory. In that zone, regional Canucks games on Sportsnet Pacific are blacked out to protect local rights unless you subscribe to Sportsnet+ Premium or NHL Centre Ice.

Why am I getting a Sportsnet blackout or NHL regional blackout tonight?

If your screen goes dark and displays a blackout message on Sportsnet or TSN, the game is a Regional Broadcast and your physical location is outside the team's NHL-defined territory. You must use Sportsnet+ Premium to stream out-of-market regional games.

Even if you pay for the channel through a standard TV package, the NHL forces the network to blackout the signal for out-of-market viewers.

How do I watch Vancouver Canucks games in Canada without regional blackouts?

Out-of-market fans living outside the team's regional territory can watch all regional Vancouver Canucks games on Sportsnet Pacific and by subscribing to Sportsnet+ Premium or NHL Centre Ice. This bypasses the standard blackout restrictions for standard cable subscribers.

Are Saturday night Vancouver Canucks games blacked out anywhere in Canada?

No. Saturday night Vancouver Canucks games on Sportsnet and TVA Sports are part of the NHL's national broadcast package. These games clear coast-to-coast across Canada with zero regional blackouts on standard cable packages.

Which Vancouver Canucks games stream exclusively on Amazon Prime Video?

National Wednesday Night Hockey broadcasts featuring the Vancouver Canucks are exclusive to Amazon Prime Video in English across Canada. These streams carry no regional blackout restrictions for Prime subscribers anywhere in the country.
