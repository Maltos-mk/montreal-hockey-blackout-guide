Calgary Flames Hockey Broadcast Rights & Regional Blackout Guide (2026–27 Flames Season)

Following the Calgary Flames regular season and preseason broadcasts across Canada requires understanding how NHL broadcast territory rights are allocated between national networks and regional partners. For Calgary Flames fans, games are split across five distinct television and streaming networks: Sportsnet West, , Sportsnet, Amazon Prime Video, and TVA Sports.

Regional Calgary Flames Games (Sportsnet West & )

Sportsnet West (31 English games) hold the official regional rights for the Calgary Flames. Their broadcast boundary is restricted by NHL carriage rules strictly to Alberta, Saskatchewan, NWT.

If you reside outside this territory, standard cable subscriptions to Sportsnet West will show a regional blackout screen during live Calgary Flames games. Out-of-market Flames fans must use Sportsnet+ Premium (for online streaming) or add NHL Centre Ice (for traditional cable/satellite TV) to watch regional feeds legally. Note that NHL Centre Ice does not include an online streaming option.

National Flames Games (Sportsnet, Prime & TVA Sports)

When the Calgary Flames play on Saturday night Hockey Night in Canada broadcasts, Sportsnet (English) and TVA Sports (French) broadcast coast-to-coast with zero regional blackouts anywhere in Canada. Every Canadian viewer with standard cable or basic streaming packages can watch these matchups.

Select regular-season Calgary Flames games stream nationally on Wednesday nights exclusively on Amazon Prime Video in English, which also carries coast-to-coast clearance with no regional blackouts.

Quick Summary for Flames Fans Living Outside Alberta/Saskatchewan: If tonight's Calgary Flames game is scheduled on Sportsnet, Prime Video, or CBC, you can watch anywhere in Canada without blackouts. If the game is on Sportsnet West, you will be blacked out in BC, Manitoba, and Eastern Canada unless you have Sportsnet+ Premium or NHL Centre Ice.

Why are Flames games blacked out in British Columbia?
The Flames and Oilers share a massive broadcast territory that covers all of Alberta, Saskatchewan, Nunavut, and the Northwest Territories. If you live in British Columbia, you are in the Canucks' home territory. In that zone, regional Flames games on Sportsnet West are blacked out to protect local rights unless you subscribe to Sportsnet+ Premium or NHL Centre Ice.

Why am I getting a Sportsnet blackout or NHL regional blackout tonight?

If your screen goes dark and displays a blackout message on Sportsnet or TSN, the game is a Regional Broadcast and your physical location is outside the team's NHL-defined territory. You must use Sportsnet+ Premium to stream out-of-market regional games.

Even if you pay for the channel through a standard TV package, the NHL forces the network to blackout the signal for out-of-market viewers.

How do I watch Calgary Flames games in Canada without regional blackouts?

Out-of-market fans living outside the team's regional territory can watch all regional Calgary Flames games on Sportsnet West and by subscribing to Sportsnet+ Premium or NHL Centre Ice. This bypasses the standard blackout restrictions for standard cable subscribers.

Are Saturday night Calgary Flames games blacked out anywhere in Canada?

No. Saturday night Calgary Flames games on Sportsnet and TVA Sports are part of the NHL's national broadcast package. These games clear coast-to-coast across Canada with zero regional blackouts on standard cable packages.

Which Calgary Flames games stream exclusively on Amazon Prime Video?

National Wednesday Night Hockey broadcasts featuring the Calgary Flames are exclusive to Amazon Prime Video in English across Canada. These streams carry no regional blackout restrictions for Prime subscribers anywhere in the country.
