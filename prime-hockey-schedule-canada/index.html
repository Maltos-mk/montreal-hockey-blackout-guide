Frequently Asked Questions

Are Amazon Prime Video hockey games blacked out in any Canadian province?

No. Every game carried on Amazon Prime Video in Canada is a national broadcast with coast-to-coast clearance. There are zero regional blackouts regardless of which province or territory you live in.

Can I watch Prime exclusive hockey games on Sportsnet or TSN cable?

No. In English across Canada, these national broadcasts are exclusive to Amazon Prime Video and do not air on linear Sportsnet or TSN cable channels. However, French-language feeds for Montreal Canadiens and Ottawa Senators games still air on RDS or TVA Sports.

Do I need an extra sports add-on to watch hockey on Amazon Prime in Canada?

No extra sports add-on is required. All national Prime hockey broadcasts in Canada are included at no additional cost with a standard Amazon Prime membership or 30-day free trial.
