The NHL Blackout & Rights Architecture Explained

Why are 50 TSN2 games blacked out if I live in Toronto or Calgary?
Bell Media (TSN and RDS) only negotiated and purchased the regional broadcast rights for Quebec, Atlantic Canada, and Eastern Ontario (Pembroke, Ottawa, Cornwall). If you live anywhere west of that boundary, the NHL strictly forbids TSN and RDS from broadcasting Habs games to your television, because they want to protect the Maple Leafs, Senators, Oilers, and Flames home broadcast markets.

Does having a cable box bypass this?
No. Blackouts originated on cable. Cable and satellite providers (Rogers, Bell, Shaw Direct, Cogeco) read your postal code and will place a static blackout screen on TSN2 or RDS during game time unless you pay for NHL Centre Ice.

Why are Saturday nights never blacked out?
Saturday night games are National rights owned by Rogers (Sportsnet) and TVA Sports. National rights carry coast-to-coast clearance across all Canadian territories with zero regional blackouts.

What about tonight's preseason split-squad game?
Tonight's Bell Centre game is on TSN2 (Montreal territory), while the Scotiabank Arena game is on TSN4 (Toronto territory). If you live in the GTA, TSN4 is in-market for you, but TSN2 is regionally blacked out.
