Why are NHL games blacked out in Canada?

If you've ever tuned in to watch your team tonight only to be met with a blank screen and a Sportsnet blackout or NHL regional blackout message, you aren't alone. The NHL's broadcasting rights in Canada are governed by a complex set of regional television boundaries.

The 84-game schedule is split into two categories: National Games and Regional Games.

National games (like Hockey Night in Canada, or the new Amazon Prime broadcasts) are available to everyone coast-to-coast without restriction. However, Regional games are strictly geofenced. For example, if a network like TSN5 owns the regional rights to the Ottawa Senators, only people physically located within the Senators' designated territory (Eastern Ontario, Quebec, Maritimes) are allowed to watch that channel.
