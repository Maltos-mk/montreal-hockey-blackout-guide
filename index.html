Montreal Canadiens Hockey Broadcast Rights & Regional Blackout Guide (2026–27 Habs Season)

Following the Montreal Canadiens (Habs) regular season and preseason broadcasts across Canada requires understanding how NHL broadcast territory rights are allocated between national networks and regional partners. For Montreal Canadiens fans, games are split across five distinct television and streaming networks: TSN2, RDS, Sportsnet, Amazon Prime Video, and TVA Sports.

Regional Montreal Canadiens Games (TSN2 & RDS)

TSN2 (50 English games) and RDS (45 French games) hold the official regional rights for the Montreal Canadiens. Their broadcast boundary is restricted by NHL carriage rules strictly to Quebec, Atlantic Canada (New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, PEI, Newfoundland), and Eastern Ontario (Pembroke, Ottawa, Cornwall).

If you reside outside this territory (such as Toronto, the GTA, Northern Ontario, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta, or British Columbia), standard cable subscriptions to TSN or RDS will show a regional blackout screen during live Montreal Canadiens games. Out-of-market Habs fans must use Sportsnet+ Premium or add NHL Centre Ice to watch regional feeds legally.

National Habs Games (Sportsnet, Prime & TVA Sports)

When the Montreal Canadiens play on Saturday night Hockey Night in Canada broadcasts, Sportsnet (English) and TVA Sports (French) broadcast coast-to-coast with zero regional blackouts anywhere in Canada. Every Canadian viewer with standard cable or basic streaming packages can watch these matchups.

Select regular-season Montreal Canadiens games stream nationally on Monday nights exclusively on Amazon Prime Video in English, which also carries coast-to-coast clearance with no regional blackouts.

Quick Summary for Habs Fans Living Outside Quebec: If tonight's Montreal Canadiens game is scheduled on Sportsnet, Prime Video, or TVA Sports, you can watch anywhere in Canada without blackouts. If the game is on TSN2 or RDS, you will be blacked out in Western Ontario and the West unless you have Sportsnet+ Premium or NHL Centre Ice.

Montreal Canadiens Broadcast & Blackout Principles

Why are Montreal Canadiens games blacked out on TSN2 in Ontario?
TSN2 and RDS own regional broadcast rights, which only cover the Montreal Canadiens' home broadcast territory (Quebec, Atlantic Canada, and Eastern Ontario including Pembroke, Ottawa, and Cornwall). If you live outside this region (such as Toronto, GTA, or Western Canada), regional licenses do not grant clearance for those games, protecting local home-market rights for teams like the Maple Leafs, Senators, Jets, Flames, Oilers, and Canucks.

Does having a standard cable box prevent Habs blackouts?
No. Blackout rules apply directly to standard cable and satellite subscriptions. If your cable billing address is in Toronto, Winnipeg, or Vancouver, your screen will display a blackout notice during live regional Montreal Canadiens games on TSN2 or RDS.

How can out-of-market Habs fans watch without blackouts?
The official method to watch blacked-out regional Montreal Canadiens games across Canada is the out-of-market package: Sportsnet+ Premium for streaming, or the NHL Centre Ice subscription package through your digital cable provider.

Why are Saturday night Habs games never blacked out?
Saturday night games are part of the National rights package on Sportsnet and TVA Sports. National broadcasts carry coast-to-coast clearance without regional blackouts across Canada.
